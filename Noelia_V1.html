The Challenges Families Face

Too often, children with disabilities are left behind.

In regions like Bolivia, families often face this challenge alone, without the resources they need to support their loved ones. Children and adults with disabilities face numerous barriers in education, healthcare, essential services, social exclusion, and career opportunities.

With over 19 million people living with disabilities in Latin America and the Caribbean, giving is paramount.

Our Promise to You

Our Mission in Action

At Noelia, our mission goes beyond addressing immediate needs — we are working to transform what is possible for children with disabilities and the families who support them.

Why We Exist

Changing the trajectory.

Across Bolivia and Latin America, too many children are left without access to essential therapies, trained professionals, and community-based support systems. The result is not just delayed care — it's lost opportunity.

Noelia exists to change that trajectory. We provide and strengthen access to critical therapies, including speech and developmental support, ensuring that children and young adults receive the care they need to communicate and learn.

By focusing on early and consistent intervention, we help unlock each child's potential and improve long-term outcomes.

Mission in Action

Three pillars that drive everything we do.

01 — Access

Expanding Access to Essential Services

We provide and strengthen access to critical therapies including speech and developmental support, ensuring children and young adults receive the care they need to communicate and learn.

02 — Capacity

Building Local Capacity Through Training

Noelia invests in training professionals and community members through a "train-the-trainer" model, equipping educators, caregivers, and service providers with lasting skills.

03 — Community

Connecting Families to Community Resources

We connect families with vital resources, support networks, and guidance — reducing isolation and empowering them to advocate for their loved ones.

Governance

Built on trust and transparency.

Noelia Foundation is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit. All donations are fully tax-deductible, and our financial oversight ensures every dollar reaches the programs that need it most.

We publish annual impact reports and provide direct access for corporate partners who want full visibility into how funds are used.

Our Programs

Where care meets community.

Our programs are designed to meet children with disabilities and their families where they are — providing essential care, building local capacity, and creating pathways to long-term inclusion.

Program 01 — Therapeutic Services

Therapeutic Services

Access to consistent, high-quality therapy can change the trajectory of a child — but for many families, it remains out of reach.

Noelia brings speech and developmental therapies to the communities that need them most. These services focus on improving communication, motor skills, and cognitive development, helping children gain independence and confidence over time.

By prioritizing early and ongoing intervention, we support meaningful progress that extends far beyond the therapy setting and into everyday life.

Learn More →
Program 02 — Community Resource Support

Community Resource Support

Families often face overwhelming barriers when trying to access care, education, and basic support systems for loved ones with disabilities.

Our community resource program connects families to essential services, guidance, and support networks within their communities. From navigating healthcare systems to accessing educational opportunities, we help families find the tools and information they need to advocate for their children.

Learn More →
Program 03 — Training & Capacity Building

Training & Capacity Building

Through our "train-the-trainer" model, Noelia equips educators, healthcare providers, and community leaders with the skills and knowledge needed to support children with disabilities.

By investing in local professionals, we expand the reach of care and ensure that support continues long after direct services are delivered. Sustainable impact requires local expertise — and that's exactly what we build.

Stories of Impact

Real children. Real change.

Education · Santa Cruz
Sofia, age 7

When Sofia arrived at our center, she hadn't spoken a word. Today, she reads aloud to her classmates and is learning to write her name.

"She laughs now. Every single day, she laughs." — Sofia's mother

Therapy · Cochabamba
Miguel, age 11

Miguel has cerebral palsy. Through six months of dedicated physical therapy, he took his first independent steps. His goal is to walk to school on his own.

"I didn't know help like this existed." — Miguel's father

Outreach · Beni Region
Valentina's Family

Valentina lives four hours by boat from the nearest road. Today she has an individualized education plan — the first child in her village to have one.

"We never thought anyone would come all the way out here." — Community elder

Build and Grow Project

Noelia is excited to continue our Build and Grow Project. Falling under our Community Resource Support program and leveraging our community ties in Santa Cruz, Bolivia, we will continue to build and remodel community centers and schools dedicated to children and young adults with disabilities.
