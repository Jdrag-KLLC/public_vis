The Challenges Families Face

Too often, children with disabilities are left behind.

In regions like Bolivia, families often face this challenge alone, without the resources they need to support their loved ones. Children and adults with disabilities face numerous barriers in education, healthcare, essential services, social exclusion, and career opportunities.

Noelia Foundation is headquartered in Fairfax, VA with active programs across Latin America, where over 19 million people live with disabilities and access to care remains critically limited.

Our Promise to You

Our Story

At Noelia, our mission goes beyond addressing immediate needs — we are working to transform what is possible for children and young adults with disabilities and the families who support them.

Founder's Story

Founded on personal experience. Built for lasting change.

Osman Cuadros, founder of Noelia, established the organization to give back to children, young adults, and families who need support most. As a father of a child with a disability, Osman has a deeply personal understanding of the daily challenges these families face.

Noelia is more than a nonprofit — it is a lifeline, providing access to critical support systems that uplift and empower communities across Latin America.

Noelia Foundation is a registered U.S. 501(c)(3) nonprofit, headquartered in Fairfax, VA, and governed by a dedicated board of directors committed to financial transparency, responsible stewardship, and scalable impact.

Mission in Action

Three pillars that drive everything we do.

01 — Access

Expanding Access to Essential Services

We provide and strengthen access to critical therapies, including speech and developmental support, ensuring that children and young adults receive the care they need to communicate and learn. Early and consistent intervention unlocks each child's potential.

02 — Capacity

Building Local Capacity Through Training

Sustainable impact requires local expertise. Noelia invests in training professionals and community members through a "train-the-trainer" model, equipping educators, caregivers, and service providers with the skills needed to support children and young adults with disabilities.

03 — Community

Connecting Families to Community Centers & Resources

Families are at the heart of everything we do. We connect them with vital support systems, resources, and community centers with therapeutic services — creating a more inclusive and supportive environment for a better quality of life.

Our Programs

Where care meets community.

At Noelia, our programs are designed to meet children with disabilities and their families where they are — providing essential care, building local capacity, and creating pathways to long-term inclusion. Explore our three programs: Therapeutic Services, Community Centers and Resource Support, and our Training and Capacity Building Program.

Program 01 — Therapeutic Services

Therapeutic Services

Access to consistent, high-quality therapy can change the trajectory of a child — but for many families, it remains out of reach.

Noelia brings speech and developmental therapies to the communities that need them most. These services focus on improving communication, motor skills, and cognitive development, helping children gain independence and confidence over time.

By prioritizing early and ongoing intervention, we support meaningful progress that extends far beyond the therapy setting and into everyday life.

Learn More →
Program 02 — Community Centers & Resource Support

Community Centers & Resource Support

Families often face overwhelming barriers when trying to access care, education, and basic support systems for loved ones with disabilities. Noelia specializes in building, remodeling, and staffing community centers with various therapeutic services to help bridge that gap.

Currently, Noelia operates a community center in Santa Cruz, Bolivia, with a roadmap for replication of similar centers across underserved communities in Latin America as our partner network and funding capacity grow.

In tandem with our community centers, we leverage local grassroots initiatives to connect families to essential services, guidance, and support networks. From navigating healthcare systems to accessing educational opportunities, we help families find the tools they need to advocate for their children.

Details on the Santa Cruz center — size, people supported, in-house expertise, and founding date — coming soon.

Learn More →
Program 03 — Training & Capacity Building

Training & Capacity Building

Through our "train-the-trainer" model, Noelia equips educators, healthcare providers, and community leaders with the skills and knowledge needed to support children with disabilities.

By investing in local professionals, we expand the reach of care and ensure that support continues long after direct services are delivered. Sustainable impact requires local expertise — and that's exactly what we build.

Education · Santa Cruz
Sofia, age 7

When Sofia arrived at our center, she hadn't spoken a word. Today, she reads aloud to her classmates and is learning to write her name.

Live Projects

Build and Grow Project

Noelia is excited to continue our Build and Grow Project. Falling under our Community Centers and Resource Support Program, we have built our first therapeutic community center for children and young adults with disabilities in Santa Cruz, Bolivia. Our goal is to build, remodel, and staff many more community centers dedicated to children and young adults with disabilities throughout Latin America.
